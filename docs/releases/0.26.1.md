## Português

- **O OwCLI responde com o Ternary Bonsai 2 e o Qwen3.8.** Com esses modelos o agente mostrava "We're currently experiencing high demand, which may cause temporary errors" a cada mensagem, mesmo com o modelo carregado e funcionando no chat. Não era demanda: o motor recusava o pedido, porque o modelo aceita uma única mensagem de sistema, no começo, e o agente manda várias. Agora o app as reúne antes de o pedido chegar ao motor. Testado com o Bonsai, numa saudação e num turno em que o agente lê um arquivo.
- **Os modelos do 9router funcionam no OwCLI logo depois de o 9router subir.** Antes, o erro "401 Unauthorized: Missing API key" aparecia quando o 9router acabava de ser instalado: a chave dele só existe depois do primeiro boot, e o app montava a lista de modelos antes disso e não a refazia. Agora a fonte sem chave fica de fora, e o app tenta de novo a cada poucos segundos até a chave aparecer.

## English

- **OwCLI answers with Ternary Bonsai 2 and Qwen3.8.** With these models the agent showed "We're currently experiencing high demand, which may cause temporary errors" on every message, even with the model loaded and working in chat. It was not demand: the engine refused the request, because the model accepts a single system message, at the start, and the agent sends several. The app now merges them before the request reaches the engine. Tested with Bonsai, on a greeting and on a turn where the agent reads a file.
- **9router models work in OwCLI right after 9router starts.** Before, the "401 Unauthorized: Missing API key" error showed up when 9router had just been installed: its key only exists after its first boot, and the app built the model list before that and never redid it. Now a source without a key is left out, and the app tries again every few seconds until the key shows up.
